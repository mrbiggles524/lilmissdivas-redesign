About Us

Long Island's most fabulous glam & spa party company

Welcome to Lil Miss Divas — where every little girl's dream of a magical spa and glam party comes to life. We specialize in fabulous spa experiences for kids ages 3 to 15, infusing every celebration with excitement, joy, and a touch of glamour.

From sparkling mini-manicures and refreshing facials to glamorous hairstyling and fabulous nail art — every detail shines. Safety and fun are our top priorities: each party is age-appropriate so every guest feels comfortable and engaged.

Whether it's a birthday, milestone, or just a day of glam fun, we transform ordinary celebrations into extraordinary memories filled with giggles, creativity, and sparkle.
